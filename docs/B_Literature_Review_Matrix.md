### 2. Synthesized Research Gap
Existing educational and conversational AI tutors exhibit three fatal technical flaws:
1. **Blind Retrieval Heuristics:** Current systems equate high dense cosine similarity with factual truth, failing completely when faced with near-miss distractor passages that contain similar vocabulary but express contradictory or irrelevant propositions.
2. **Uncalibrated Refusal Thresholds:** Fixed heuristic thresholds (e.g., $\text{sim} < 0.50$) fail under domain shifts, producing overconfidence on out-of-syllabus questions and over-abstention on complex valid questions.
3. **Absence of Course-Specific Attribution:** Prior academic benchmarks (Natural Questions, TriviaQA) focus on broad encyclopedic facts rather than structured graduate syllabus knowledge containing explicit mathematical conditions and derivations.

---

### 3. Falsifiable Research Hypothesis
Based on the identified gaps, we formulate the following formal hypothesis:

$$\mathcal{H}_1: \text{Augmenting dense retrieval with a cross-encoder NLI evidence-agreement verifier and temperature-scaled conformal abstention thresholding achieves significantly higher calibrated abstention AUROC (} \Delta \text{AUROC} \ge 0.15 \text{) and reduces ungrounded hallucination rate by } \ge 70\% \text{ compared to standard thresholded RAG baselines on course-specific out-of-scope evaluations, without degrading in-syllabus answer accuracy.}$$
